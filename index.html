About Me

Research with purpose.
Technology with impact.

A multidisciplinary academic and technology professional focused on translating research into usable, sustainable, and scalable solutions.

I am Dr. Edgar Bryan B. Nicart, DIT, an academic professional affiliated with the College of Computing and Multimedia Studies at the University of Camarines Norte (UCN), formerly the Camarines Norte State College.

My professional interests span artificial intelligence, deep learning, computer vision, data analytics, software engineering, educational technology, and digital transformation. I am particularly interested in applying these disciplines to agricultural productivity, institutional decision-making, community development, and inclusive innovation.

My work also involves research coordination, project implementation, publication development, and knowledge management. I value technology that is not only technically sound but also understandable, accessible, and useful to the people it is intended to serve.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration, academic partnerships, software and AI development, consultancy, and research dissemination.
